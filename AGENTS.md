## Preserve the distinctions

- Keep Cosmic / Social / Existential sovereign Fields distinct from Consciousness / Mental / Physical representations of one situated condition.
- An intention represents an attempt; it cannot author another person's choice or guarantee actualisation.
- Power is relational becoming, not a stored strength, influence or truth score. Force and Flow remain distinct expressions.
- Write trace after the producing event. History affects later possibility; do not fabricate a same-Moment cause.
- Delivered words, received interpretation, retained history and truth about a referent are distinct. Representational consequence alone creates no Physical productive surplus.
- Preserve finite material support, physiological mediation and physical-time rates. Do not lower gates to force Will, Praxis, cooperation or institutions to appear.
- The legacy contribution barrier and new timed control turns are app protocols, not ontological global turns.
- Preserve the clear original goal. Human players decide developing ambitions and commitments; do not invent a consolation victory.
