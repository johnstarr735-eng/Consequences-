# Consequences — the notebooks and DESTINY come first
18 October 2026

## Governing direction

The notebooks supply the philosophical questions, distinctions and source material. DESTINY turns a reviewed interpretation of those sources into capacities and causal processes. Consequences lets two people act within that world and encounter what follows.

The design question is: **given this situation and its history, what can become possible, what can be attempted, and what actually changes when things meet?**

A scenario supplies starting circumstances and a clear game objective. It does not override bodies, access, other people's agency, material support or recorded history to make a desired story happen.

This direction is recorded in [the working instructions](../AGENTS.md). The [philosophy review](DESTINY_PHILOSOPHY_REVIEW.md) records what is active and what remains partial; its findings remain the implementation baseline.

## How a mechanic earns its place

| Piece | What to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Notebook source | A located passage and its context. Identify manuscript versus later commentary, metaphor versus explicit theoretical claim, and unresolved tensions. |
| DESTINY mechanism | The capacities, conditions, encounters and retained history that could cause the effect. Identify the active integration path and any missing prerequisite. |
| Playable consequence | A falsifiable example: changing a relevant circumstance should change what can be attempted or what follows, without scripting a preferred outcome. |

Keep exact rates, thresholds, scene scales and simplifications labelled as engineering inference. The notebooks do not supply every coefficient. The engine is an interpretation that can also contain omissions.

A new command synonym, interface or language model does not by itself add a capacity to the world. Diagnose the missing mechanism before promising that it is supported.

## Philosophical commitments

- One situated condition has distinct Consciousness, Mental and Physical representations. Cosmic, Social and Existential Fields are a separate axis.
- Moira concerns situated carrying limits, not predestination or a single character score.
- Will is finite orientation before actualisation. Imagination can represent a configuration that does not yet obtain. Praxis must answer to actual consequences.
- Power is the actuality of relational becoming. It does not certify truth or supply an owned bonus. Force and Flow remain distinct expressions.
- Events leave appropriately attributed trace after resolution. The changed situation changes later possibility.
- Acknowledgement and interpretation can matter without manufacturing a Physical referent or productive surplus.
- Bodies and materials remain finite. Rest is not fuel; representation is not material production.
- Harmful acts remain attempts. Resistance, access, surviving bodies, witnesses and later meetings govern consequences; a universal moral penalty does not replace those mechanisms.
- The original goal stays clear. Players develop commitments and ambitions through play without the app deciding their values.

| 2. Human intention and finite Will | Reconcile geometric player orientation with intended configurations and canonical finite Will. | Circumstances can support, interrupt or defeat the same intention. It supplies no Force or control over another person's choice. |
| 3. Imagination and practical feedback | Connect a represented possibility, an actual attempt, encountered error and supported later revision. | A failed attempt informs a later attempt without granting free skill or making an imagined relation physically actual. |
| 4. Relational capacity | Return to the notebooks and the savepoint's unresolved question of what a Relation can become capable of in each reality. | A new capacity is earned through the right history, not assigned by a friendship, authority or cooperation label. |
| 5. Further scenarios and presentation | Expand as supported capacities become playable. Continue bounded reliability, accessibility and device work alongside this. | New scenarios use the same causal world rather than separate authored outcome systems. |

Do not lower engine gates to force emergence. The review's zero Will/Imagination/Praxis episodes in three complete investigations is a diagnosis to investigate, not a statistic to tune away.

## Current implementation boundary

App 0.8.1 repairs naming, action attribution/recaps, response-time display, device setup review and clean installation; see [the gameplay review](GAMEPLAY_REVIEW_0_8_1.md). These are integration and reliability corrections, not new canonical mechanisms.

The intended environment selector is **historical / fantasy / present as generation modes**. Connected AI should generate a structured initial world; supported processes validate and govern its capacities, and the accepted conditions/history persist. The three prepared profiles in 0.8 are demonstration environments and do not yet fulfil that direction. AI interpretation and generation must operate within the saved world rather than authoring outcomes or new laws on demand.

App 0.8.0 adds [timed worlds and character setup](TIMED_WORLDS.md): fifteen-minute sequential control windows with multiple prompts, finite response opportunities, persisted ongoing activity, three supported environment profiles, bounded character histories and validated scenario recipes. This protocol supersedes the contribution barrier for new matches only. Initial-history handling and observation remain app calibrations; they are not fabricated canonical Praxis or a complete practical-learning system.


App 0.7.0 adds the first [situated-agency implementation](SITUATED_AGENCY.md): reusable physical world definitions, supported attention over time, private purpose/self-account, reached introductions, and an explicit spatial bridge into canonical Will with its original formation gates. These are bounded integrations; general social recognition, nonspatial reflective configurations and the canonical app-encounter trace bridge remain incomplete.

App 0.6.0 begins the social-environment work in [SOCIAL_ENVIRONMENT.md](SOCIAL_ENVIRONMENT.md): a saved present-day setting, finite local officer, separate supported communications routes and individual role histories. Delivered reports can change the officer's actual movement and later encounters. Detailed communications remain app-level history; this does not close the canonical attribution or Will/Praxis gaps below.

The runtime uses the canonical V1.13 dependency closure from the supplied 20 September savepoint, plus identified app adapters. It is not automatically the latest later DESTINY lineage.

The notebook corpus informs source review and implementation. The application does **not** retrieve the full manuscripts or consult them live for each turn. The optional language interpreter, currently unconnected, interprets attempts within implemented capacities; it cannot supply missing physics or social causation.

Finite bodies, material constraints, access, provenance and some retained consequences are active. Human intention, social memory and many app incidents remain only partly connected to canonical Will and relational feedback. The alpha's finite operation vocabulary remains a real limitation. Putting the sources first means working through these gaps and reporting them honestly.
