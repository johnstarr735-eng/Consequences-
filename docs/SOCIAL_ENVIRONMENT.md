# Consequences — the first social environment

17 October 2026 · app 0.6.0 · environment profile 1

## Working setting

New Night Watch matches take place at noon on 17 October 2026, at the harbour observatory in **Port Serein**, a fictional coastal municipality. Present day is a working choice for this first playable profile, not a period selected by the user. The scenario establishes the environment before either player contributes an intention and saves its identity, institutions, role histories, infrastructure, devices and pending communications with the match.

The environment supplies the concrete situation in which the Social Field is expressed. It is not a replacement definition of that sovereign Field, an extra Field, or a society-wide pressure score.

## What a communication does

The interpreter now accepts `Call police: Please check on Nell`, `Text Jonah: Meet me at the case desk`, and `Message Jonah: Check the recorder`. `Message` uses internet service; `Text` uses SMS. `Text signal log to Jonah` transmits a record the sender has acquired, retaining its original root and provenance. These commands work without the optional language-model connection.

An attempt requires a conscious operator, learned handset procedure, manipulation, control of a functioning unlocked device, sufficient charge, a retained recipient address and a working outgoing route. A voice attempt additionally requires speech. Knowing someone's name does not provide their number. The first profile supplies the two investigators' numbers and the local duty line through their opening introduction; the other inhabitants have no invented handset address.

Sending creates a saved message in transit. At the delivery boundary, the receiving device and route must work. Delivery to a device is separate from a conscious person reading or hearing its contents. Texts can wait in the handset while their owner is unconscious or lacks control of it. A call can go unanswered. This slice fails a transmission to an unavailable receiving device rather than simulating a carrier's store-and-forward service. It does not implement voicemail or a general telephone conversation agent.

Outgoing call words are spoken locally and can be overheard under the existing hearing conditions. Typed messages are not broadcast to nearby people. A recipient's private view acquires content only after reading/hearing it. Sender receipts establish at most delivery to the handset, not the person's belief or agreement. Acquired copies keep their source identity; a fresh assertion about a crime supplies no original material proof. No communication mints Physical stock or productive surplus.

## A report becomes a situated response

The causal route is:

1. Someone produces words through a supported channel or speaks within the officer's hearing.
2. The reached officer retains those actual words and their source/channel/time.
3. A bounded scenario policy identifies a safety/check request and a named encountered person, or the sender when no such person is named. It chooses the first person mentioned, not a hidden culprit or a semantic finding of guilt.
4. The officer attempts to reach that person's retained position. Current position can update only through actual sensory access. Navigation uses the same bodily movement and obstacle constraints as the investigators.
5. Arrival supplies an opportunity to encounter the situation. It does not prove the earlier claim or produce a conviction.
6. If the officer encounters ongoing harmful contact and reaches it, their body can add resistance through the existing intervention process. The uniform supplies no extra Force.

A false report can therefore redirect a real, finite person without becoming true. A broken channel can leave that possibility unrealised. A nearby officer and an absent officer can produce different contact outcomes. A late witness encounters the ongoing contact without being credited with its unobserved beginning. Immediate intervention, later testimony and prior truth remain distinct.

The dispatch and intervention policies are app-level hypotheses, not general emergent police behaviour, human values, learned deference or jurisdictional law. Reports are handled in received order, with the latest qualifying report becoming the current check. No severity classifier, autonomous arrest, search warrant, forced confession or authority-based obedience is implemented.

## Implementation and calibration

`lib/case-environment.mjs` holds scenario creation, channel prerequisites, delivery, retained recipient words, report checking and filtered public/self views. `case-world.mjs` creates the actual officer/router/handsets; the phones use canonical physical couplings. The culprit roster remains the three original witnesses, separate from the complete inhabitant roster. `case-simulation.mjs` splits physical integration at pending delivery boundaries and gives the officer actual navigation attempts. Existing contact processes now include the officer and can register a newly arriving witness.

Engineering choices: a 0.5-minute transit interval; a 3.2-scene-unit local WiFi radius; an electronic working-integrity gate of 0.6; starting handset charge 0.85; idle charge expenditure 0.00015 per simulated minute; voice expenditure 0.003 and text/data expenditure 0.001 per attempted transmission. These dimensionless rates are not notebook laws or hardware measurements. They are stable in physical time rather than per solver frame. National coverage, electrical generation and telecom operators remain declared external support within this small scene.

The canonical 23 V1.13 runtime files remain byte-identical to the source manifest. The new people, material positions and encountered motion participate in the existing kernel. Detailed messages and institutional interpretation are still app-level retained state; they are **not yet a general canonical Mental relational-trace bridge**. Will/Imagination/Praxis integration and the broader encounter-attribution gap documented in the [review](DESTINY_PHILOSOPHY_REVIEW.md) remain open. The initial profile supplies human work histories; it does not demonstrate their emergence.

New profile matches use state format 3. Existing saves are not retrospectively given a new officer, handset or political history. The graph codec preserves the new profile, role histories, device controls/charge, queued delivery, inboxes, received reports and assignments. Existing immutable D1 chunk storage handles the additional retained encounter volume; the standard four-exchange investigation's compressed-size guard is now 3 MB for six inhabitants and the new material Forms, rather than 1.8 MB for five.

## Falsification checks

`tests/social-environment.test.mjs` contrasts supported and absent channels, working and unavailable devices, reading and incapacity, nearby and absent physical intervention, pending and delivered reports, original proof and assertion, and continuous versus resumed state. It checks that identical physical time at different integration step sizes gives the same delivery time and device idle cost. The existing full investigation fixtures still establish the original objective for all three prior culprit histories.

Broader regimes, new social practices and authority mechanisms should follow this same standard: a source locator, an actual support/encounter/history path, explicit calibration and a contrasting fixture that could falsify the proposed consequence. A new environment label alone is insufficient.
